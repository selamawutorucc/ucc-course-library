--- PAGE 7 ---
Certifications vs digital badges
Both matter, but they do different things.
Professional certification
Usually broader, exam-based and 
recognised by employers or industry 
bodies.
Examples: CCNA, Security+, AWS Cloud Practitioner, 
Azure Fundamentals, ISC2 CC
Digital badge
Usually smaller and skill-specific: a 
course, lab, learning path or practical 
achievement.
Examples: Microsoft Learn badges, Cisco NetAcad 
badges, AWS Skill Builder, Google Cloud Skills Boost

The role of AI in your career
Use AI as a learning assistant, not as a replacement for thinking.
✓
Use AI for support
Code explanation, debugging 
assistance, documentation, 
brainstorming, testing and prototyping.
!
Still understand the work
The code, algorithm, security 
implications, accuracy, bias, testing and 
ethics.
→
Strong message
Do not allow AI to replace your thinking 
while you are learning how to think 
computationally.

CSC101: Introduction to Computing (3 Credit Hour)
• Course Description
• Explores essential concepts in computer systems, hardware, software, 
data representation, and operating systems.
• Covers the basics of computer networks, the Internet, and cloud 
computing architectures.
• Introduces computational thinking, algorithms, and foundational 
programming concepts to solve real-world problems.
• Examines databases, information systems, cybersecurity principles, 
artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies.
• Analyzes the ethical and societal impacts of technology while building 
critical skills needed for advanced CS coursework.
