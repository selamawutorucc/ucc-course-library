Course Description 
This course provides an introduction to general physics and in mechanics. The content deals with: 
Introduction to mechanics, vectors, dynamics, kinematics, work and energy, elasticity, fluids and 
thermodynamics. 
 
 
 
 
 
Course Objectives: 
At the end of the course, the student should be able to: 
• 
Identify physical quantities and standard units, deduce derived quantities from the fundamentals 
and convert other units to SI units. 
• 
Differentiate between vectors and scalar quantities, identify components of vector, and apply 
mathematical operators like addition, subtraction and multiplication on vectors. 
• Define force, discuss types of forces, discuss Newton’s laws of motion, explain impulse and 
momentum, discuss inelastic and elastic collision, strings and pulleys, inclined planes. 
• Explain displacement, speed, velocity and acceleration, plot graphs of motion, and discuss the 
following types of motion :( Circular motion, simple harmonic motion and projectile motion). 
• Explain work, energy and power, derive expressions for work, kinetic energy, potential energy and 
power. 
• Explain elasticity, define Hooke’s law and describe an experiment to verify Hooke’s law, discuss 
young’s modulus, shear modulus and bulk modulus.

--- PAGE 2 ---
• Explain the state of matter, explain density and relative density, discuss pressure in liquids, describe 
an experiment to verify Pascal’s law and explain buoyancy and Archimedes principle. 
• Explain kinetic theory of gases, discuss temperature scales, explain lab and clinical thermometers, 
discuss heat capacities of matter and explain thermal conductivity. 
 
 
Mode of Teaching: 
The course is taught in two modes via face-to-face classroom setting and E-learning platform with relevant 
teaching (visual and audio-visual) aids to enhance students’ understanding of the concepts. Lectures are 
done in the form of discussion where students are allowed to express themselves in other to bring divers 
ideas about the topic under consideration which promotes intellectual flexibility.  For each group 
presentation, a leader is chosen by the members of the team and the rest support him/her. This help 
individual students to improve their leadership skills. At end of every major topic, students are given trial 
questions to solve to enable them to reflect on what have been discussed. 
 
Mode of Assessment: Two (2) quizzes which carry 10 marks each, Assignments (10 marks) and Power 
point presentations (10 marks). The created groups of students are given different topics to analyze 
critically and present in class (Power point presentation). 
Exams: The exams (60%) consist of qualitative and quantitative problems similar to those discussed in class and those 
assigned for homework.
