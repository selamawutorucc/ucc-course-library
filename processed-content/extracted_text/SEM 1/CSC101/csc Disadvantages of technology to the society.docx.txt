State the disadvantages of technology to the society
Social isolation and loneness. Due to the easy access to technology people decide to stay on their phone more than connecting with people in real life. As a cause an individual can have about a thousand friends and know just ten of in actual life this goes a long way to cause social isolation and even loneness. 
Joblessness. Due to the advancement of technologies, they are able to perform tasks humans are to perform faster and better. These have led to companies reducing the number of humans the employ and replacing them with technologies which goes round to create more joblessness situations.
Destruction. The advancement of technology likes drones, planes etc. has caused people to deliver weapons to other places which are used for destruction of lives and properties. Also technology like the 3D Printer has made it easier in the making of weapons like guns.
Addiction. Technology has made social media more accessible to the society and those who use it without control tends to be addicted hence procrastinate the work they are supposed to do. In today’s word people are more addicted to technology than it was before due to access to games movies, social media etc.
Degradation of memory. Thanks to technology we are able to save things we used to keep in our head. We save phone numbers, birthdays, schedules, locations etc. so we are able to refer to them when it starts fading from our head. However, over using it has caused the degradation since our brain does not need to remember it. 
Disadvantages of technology to the student
Extra distraction for students. Due to the presence of technologies like mobile phones and laptops, students don’t focus on what they are really supposed to do but use their devices all day. Some students don’t pay attention in class due to the usage of mobile phones.
Learning is becoming more expensive. Since the implementation of technologies many institutions are thriving to cope with the digital world hence increasing their budget making their charge for services increase. Therefore students would have to pay more fees and also get extra money for other things like bandwidth in order to cope and learn successfully.
Misguiding of information. Technology has caused students not to go to libraries for books in order to do their research. They surf the internet for information use and present them as their research work which may sometimes lead to the presentation of wrong information as correct since most websites concentrate on being well know than providing accurate information. 
Makes cheating easier. Technology has increased the rate at which students get away with cheating which cause them to graduate without adequate knowledge. A typical example is the use of artificial intelligence like ChatGPT to do assignments.
Degradation of memory. Analyzing, you would realize that students who graduated in the era when technology was less are smarter than those whom graduated in this era. This is because they had to do research themselves and keep the information the find in their heads causing their brain to adapt and improve upon its performance.
