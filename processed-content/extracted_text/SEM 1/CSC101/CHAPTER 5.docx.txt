A ______________is any event or action that could cause a loss of or damage to computer or mobile device hardware, software, data, information, or processing capability. (digital security risk)
Any illegal act involving the use of a computer or related devices generally is referred to as _______________ (Computer Crime)
______________refers to someone who accesses a computer or network illegally. (Hacker)
Someone who accesses a computer or network illegally but has the intent of destroying data, stealing information, or other malicious action is known as________________ (cracker)
A _______________has the same intent as a cracker but does not have the technical skills and knowledge. (script kiddie)
Someone who demands payment to stop an attack on an organization’s technology infrastructure is known as ________________ (cyber extortionist)
A_______________ is someone who uses the Internet or network to destroy or damage computers for political reasons. (cyber terrorist)
An attack whose goal ranges from disabling a government’s computer network to crippling a country is termed as ______________ (cyberwarfare).
_________________ consists of programs that act without a user’s knowledge and deliberately alter the operations of computers and mobile devices. (malicious software)
A group of compromised computers or mobile devices connected to a network that are used to attack other networks is known as ________________ (botnet, or zombie army)
A____________ is a program that performs a repetitive task on a network. (bot)
An assault whose purpose is to disrupt computer access to an Internet service is known as ____________  (denial of service attack (DoS attack))
_______________is a program or set of instructions in a program that allows users to bypass security controls when accessing a program, computer, or network. (back door)
A technique intruders use to make their network or Internet transmission appear legitimate to a victim computer or network is known as ________________ (Spoofing)
________________occurs when an intruder computer fools a network into believing its IP address is associated with a trusted source. (IP spoofing)
A ______________is a hardware and/or software that protects a network’s resources from intrusion (firewall)
A server outside the organization’s network that controls which communications pass in and out of the organization’s network is called a ___________________. (proxy server)
The use of a computer or network without permission is known as _________________.  (Unauthorized access)
_______________is the use of a computer or its data for unapproved or possibly illegal activities. (Unauthorized use)
_______________outlines the activities for which the computer and network may and may not be used.( Acceptable Use Policy (AUP))
__________ is a security measure that defines who can access a computer or network (access control)
__________records in a file both successful and unsuccessful access attempts.(audit trail)
A private combination of words associated with a user name that allows access to certain computer resources. (password)
A numeric password, either assigned by a company or selected by a user is known as _________. (passcode)
A possessed object is any item that you must possess, or carry with you, in order to gain access to a computer or computer facility.
A ________________authenticates a person’s identity by translating a personal characteristic, such as a fingerprint, into a digital code that is compared with a digital code stored in a computer or mobile device verifying a physical or behavioral characteristic. (biometric device)
A _______________captures curves and indentations of a fingerprint. (fingerprint reader)
____________ measures the shape and size of a person’s hand. (hand geometry system)
_______________is the discovery, collection, and analysis of evidence found on computers and networks. (Cyberforensics/digital forensics)
When someone steals software media, intentionally erases programs, illegally registers and/or activates a program, or illegally copies a program, it is termed as ___________. (Software theft)
_______________ is the unauthorized and illegal duplication of copyrighted software. Piracy is the most common form of software theft. (Piracy)
_____________occurs when someone steals personal or confidential information. (Information theft)
The process of converting data that is readable by humans into encoded characters to prevent unauthorized access is known as ____________. (Encryption)
A_____________ is an encrypted code that a person, website, or organization attaches to an electronic message to verify the identity of the message sender. (digital signature)
A website that uses encryption techniques to secure its data is known as a_____________ (secure site).
______________is the oldest copy of the file. (grandparent)
____________ is the second oldest copy of the file.(parent)
The most recent copy of the file is called a________. (Child).
___________________are the moral guidelines that govern the use of computers, mobile devices, information systems, and related technologies. (Technology ethics)
______________refers to unique and original works, such as ideas or writings.
. (Intellectual property)
A written guideline that helps determine whether a specification is ethical/ unethical or allowed/not allowed is termed as _____________. (code of conduct)
_____________refers to the right of individuals and companies to deny or restrict the collection, use, and dissemination of information about them. (Information privacy)
A____________ is a small text file that a web server stores on your computer. (cookie)
Online shopping sites generally use a____________ to keep track of items in a user’s shopping cart. (session cookie)
______________is a scam in which a perpetrator sends an official looking email message that attempts to obtain your personal and/or financial information. (Phishing)
Adware on mobile phones is known as _____________. (madware)
___________________is defined as gaining unauthorized access to or obtaining confidential information by taking advantage of the trusting human nature of some victims and the naivety of others.( social engineering)
The process of restricting access to certain material. Many businesses use content filtering to limit employees’ web access is known as ______________. (Content filtering)
______________is a program that restricts access to specified websites. Some also filter sites that use specific words (Web filtering software)
The unencrypted, readable data is called________________ (plaintext)
The encrypted, unreadable data is called _____________(ciphertext)
_____________is the act of stealing digital equipment. (Hardware theft)
_______________involves defacing or destroying digital equipment. (Hardware vandalism)
