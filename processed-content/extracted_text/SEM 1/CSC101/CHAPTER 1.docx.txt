A ________________is a portable flash memory storage device that you plug in a USB port. (USB flash drive)

___________is an Internet service that provides remote storage to computer users. (Cloud storage)

The ____________is a global library of information available to anyone connected to the Internet. (World Wide Web)
A collection of computers and devices connected together is often called a__________( Network)
The _________is a worldwide collection of computer networks that connects millions of businesses, government agencies, educational institutions, and individuals. (Internet)
___________is the process of transferring content from a server on the Internet to a computer or mobile device (Downloading)
Each electronic document on the web is called a____________ (Webpage)
A computer small enough to hold in the hand is called ____________ (Mobile device)
A ____________is a collection of related webpages, which are stored on a web server. (Website)
A ____________is a computer that delivers requested webpages to your computer or mobile device. (Web server)
___________ is a built-in connection to other documents, graphics, audio files, videos, webpages, or websites. (Hyperlink)
_______________refers to the activity of using links to explore the web. (Surfing)
When a computer or device connects to a network, it is said to be ____________. (Online)

_______________, short for malicious software, is software that acts without a user’s knowledge and deliberately alters the computer’s or mobile device’s operations (Malware)
____________involves reducing the electricity consumed and environmental waste generated when using a computer. (Green computing)
An ________________is a set of programs that coordinates all the activities among computer or mobile device hardware. (Operating system)
An __________consists of programs designed to make users more productive and/or assist them with personal tasks (Application)
A ____________is someone who develops programs and apps or writes the instructions that direct the computer or mobile device to process data into information. (Software developer)
A _________________is hardware capable of transferring items from computers and devices to transmission media and vice versa. (Communications device)
A __________is a wireless network that provides Internet connections to mobile computers and devices (Hot spot)
A system that attempts to imitate the behavior of the human brain is known as _____________________. ( Neural network)
A ___________is an informal website consisting of time-stamped articles (posts) in a diary or journal format, usually listed in reverse chronological order. (Blog)
A __________is recorded media that users can download or stream to a computer or portable media player (Podcast)
A __________is a collaborative website that allows users to create, add to, modify, or delete the content via their browser (Wiki)

A _____________ is a user who requires the capabilities of a powerful computer. (Power user)
