Objective 
This course will allow students to become productive by acquiring a basic understanding of Microsoft Word, Microsoft Excel, and Microsoft PowerPoint, and to learn to share data between these applications
Course Description 
This course uses the world’s most popular suite of business applications for productivity and efficiency. It teaches the most basic features as well as focuses on the more advanced tools that all students will find useful. The course provides business productivity application for the management and manipulation of data. It provides skills to create dynamic and professional presentations. 
Expected Outcome 
By the end of the course, students will be able to 
Understand which tasks each of the major office programs can perform.
Independently create professional-looking documents, presentations, and spreadsheets. 
Understand advanced Office functions, including Mail Merge (Word) and Formulas (Excel). 
Understand how to use Word, Excel, and PowerPoint in a variety of professional, educational and personal situations.
