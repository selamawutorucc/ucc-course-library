How do we call the space between two words
Word spacing
What is the space between two letters
Letter spacing
What is the space between two lines?
Line spacing
What is an object stroke?
This refers to the outline or border surrounding an object such as picture shape etc.
What is a text stroke?
This refers to the thin line that surrounds or outlines text characters. 
Differentiate between a gutter and a margin. 
Margins are blank spaces around a text in a document, while gutters are wider margins that run along the binding edge of a book or document.
