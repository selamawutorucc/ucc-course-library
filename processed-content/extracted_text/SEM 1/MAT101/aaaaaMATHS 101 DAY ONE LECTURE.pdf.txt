Union and Intersection of Sets 
The Union of Sets: The union of two sets say A and B written as (𝐴∪𝐵) is the set which 
consists of all the elements that belong to A or to B or to both.  
Mathematically written as 𝐴∪𝐵= {𝑥∈𝑀 𝑜𝑟 𝑥∈𝐴  𝑜𝑟 𝑥∈𝐵)

Intersection of Sets: The intersection of the sets A and B (𝐴∩𝐵) is the set which consists of 
elements that belong to both A and B. Mathematically written as 
 𝐴∩𝐵= {𝑥∈𝑀 𝑜𝑟 𝑥∈𝐴 𝑎𝑛𝑑 𝑥∈𝐵}

always belongs to S and the second member always belong to T.
