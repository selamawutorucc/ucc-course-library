--- SLIDE 3 ---
Types of Systems
Isolated systems
Energy does not cross the boundary of the system
Total energy of the system is constant
Non-isolated systems
Energy can cross the system boundary in a variety of ways.
Total energy of the system changes
Conservation of energy
Can be used if no non-conservative forces act within the isolated system
Applies to biological organisms, technological systems, engineering situations, etc

--- SLIDE 6 ---
Conservation of Energy
Energy is conserved
This means that energy cannot be created nor destroyed.
If the total amount of energy in a system changes, it can only be due to the fact that energy has crossed the boundary of the system by some method of energy transfer.

--- SLIDE 7 ---
Isolated System
For an isolated system, DEmech = 0
Remember Emech = K + U
This is conservation of energy for an isolated system with no non-conservative forces acting.

If non-conservative forces are acting, some energy is transformed into internal energy.

Isolated System, cont.

The SI unit of power is called the watt.

A unit of power in the US Customary system is horsepower.
1 hp = 746 W
