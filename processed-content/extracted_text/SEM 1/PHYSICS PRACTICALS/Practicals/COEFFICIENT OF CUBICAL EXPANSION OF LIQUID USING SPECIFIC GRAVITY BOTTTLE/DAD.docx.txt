PROCEEDURE:
The specific gravity bottle was rinsed with methylated spirit and then it was allowed to dry, to hasten the process air it blown into it. The bottle is then weighed on the electronic weighing balance. After the gravity bottle is then filled with glycerine from a beaker. The stopper is then inserted and the bottle was handled less to prevent it from warming, the outside of the gravity bottle was wiped thoroughly with a filter paper. The now filled gravity bottle is then weighed again on the electronic balance,  the temperature of the glycerine in the bottle is then taken. A cotton thread is then tied tightly and then suspended bottle from the clamp so that it is almost completely immersed in the water. It was then heated in the water – bath and then it was boiled until the temperature is a 100 degree Celsius. The bottle is then removed and the wiped thoroughly with a filter paper. It was then cooled and the bottle reweighed.

Procedure: The specific gravity bottle together with its stopper was weighed after it had been cleaned by rinsing out with methylated spirit. The mass of the cleaned, dried and empty bottle with its stopper was recorded, say, M0 . The specific gravity bottle was then filled with glycerine from a beaker in which the temperature of the glycerine at the time of filling was taken, say, t1(0C) with a thermometer. After that, the stopper of the bottle of the bottle was inserted and the overflow was wiped off completely with filter paper. It was ensured that the bottle was handled as little as possible to avoid warming it. The new mass of the specific gravity bottle was recorded, say, M1, using the electronic balance. A piece of cotton thread was then tied securely around the neck of the bottle and used to suspend it from the clamp, in a way that it was almost totally immersed in the water bath. The water bath was then heated to boil the water for some time and its temperature was taken, say, t2 (0C), with the thermometer, after which the heat was taken off. The bottle was wiped clean and allowed to cool. After cooling for sometime, the bottle was reweighed and its mass was recorded, say M2.

Discussion of errors:
Sources of error like buoyancy effect, air current acting upon the object by surrounding air caused an increase or decrease in the original value due to density of air, impure glycerine delayed expansion and reduced the boiling point of the glycerine when it was heated, and the corroded balance pan affected the accuracy of the mass.
