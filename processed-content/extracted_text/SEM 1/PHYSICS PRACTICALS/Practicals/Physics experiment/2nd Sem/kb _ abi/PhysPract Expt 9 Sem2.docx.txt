METHOD

The object pin O was positioned at a distance from the lens. SOURCES OF ERROR
Error may be due to spherical and chromatic aberrations of lens.
The search pin as well as the object pin was too loosely attached to the optical bench. 
Error caused may be due to parallax between the search pin and the image of the object pin.
Error caused may also be due to the scratches on the lens.
PRECAUTIONS
It was ensured that the pins were in no parallax position.
It was ensured that the tips of the search pins were in line with principal axis of the lens.
3. The eye was placed exactly in front of the search pin and not at an angle above or below the pins.
4. The lens was wiped thoroughly to make it clean and clear when looking through it.
5. A screen was placed behind the set up too ensure a clear view of the images formed.
6. The experiment was set up in a well lighted surrounding to produce clear images.
CONCLUSION
The focal length of the converging lens used in the experiment is 10.2cm.
