COURSE OUTLINE
Course Description
Engaging in academic work at the university is challenging.  This course is aimed at equipping fresh students to make the transition from pre-university level to the university level. It assists them in engaging and succeeding in complex academic tasks in speaking, listening, reading and writing.  It also provides an introduction to university studies by equipping students with skills that will help them to engage in academic discourse with confidence and fluency.
Objectives 
The objectives of the course are to:
help students develop functional skills in the use of English language.
enhance students’ communicative ability in English. 
equip students with skills in listening, speaking, reading and writing.
assist students pay attention to grammatical accuracy and conventions of usage.

Mode of Delivery
This course will be interactive (i.e. seminars and discussion). Students will be expected to participate fully in all class activities.
Mode of Assessment
Continuous Assessment (40%)
This is based on:
General quiz
Class assignment 
Quizzes
Class participation
Final Examination (60%)
This consists of an end-of-semester examination based on various aspects of the semester’s work. Details of the requirements for both continuous assessments and final examination will be spelt out in class.
General Course Policies
Attendance/Participation
Regular attendance at classes is required. Attendance (and participation) will influence the final grade.  Students are advised to prepare by carefully following the readings and being ready to contribute and engage in discussions.
Plagiarism
The university has rules concerning plagiarism and it is the responsibility of each student to be familiar with the said rules.  Plagiarism will result in disciplinary sanctions.
