PASSAGE ONE
That fateful morning as all are seated, the four notorious thieves together with Hakim are marched into the inner chamber of the court house. Quite a number of the court’s audience including of course Jael, Hakim’s beloved, sit on tenterhooks. Everyone recollects the events of the previous day when the story of the four musketeers was told. Apparently, the four young crooks with a plan hatched by Hakim have managed to shepherd a herd of cattle belonging to one Al-haji Bature into an articulated truck and drove it straight from Tamale to Manyenka in Kumasi and auctioned the entire herd of the beardless Al-haji for something close to a million Ghana cedis. Luckily, the entire herd of cows and bulls has been rescued from the imminent slaughter that had faced them. Not long after they have been summoned into the chamber, the sitting judge and master of their fate orders them to look him direct in the eye as he made his submission. The judge tells them that if they are bold and smart enough to plan and steal a whole herd of cattle, why are they not thinking enough to know that perhaps a cow donated to him in advance would kill their present predicament. He tells them he does not understand why Hakim, the mastermind of the group, could not see beyond his nose and realize that a judge as lean as him would accept a bribe of one cow leg in return for their freedom. During this tirade, the clerks who take notes of record for the court during all times write nothing all. None of these statements is meant for the record.
PASSAGE 2
AIDS have considerably reduced and continue to reduce the population figures of many African countries. The origin of this pandemic is still unknown, but geneticists of many a country thinks that it is the bizarre consequence of genetic engineering, One of the few geneticists who believes that this manipulation has a hidden racist agenda has been at pains to prove his point to the hilt. To him, neither the view that the disease have originated in Africa nor the widespread religious speculations that the pandemic is a divine scourge or the advent of doomsday holds water. The geneticist together with other colleagues lament that a billion dollar are being spent on some aspects of the disease that is anything but far-reaching. He contend that a global funding for the combat of AIDS should be set up.
