The Scalar or Dot Product 
  
 
 
The multiplication of a vector by a scalar was discussed in Appendix A.  When 
we multiply a vector by another vector, we must define precisely what we mean.  One 
type of vector product is called the scalar or dot product and is covered in this appendix.  
A second type of vector product is called the vector or cross product and is covered in 
Appendix C.
