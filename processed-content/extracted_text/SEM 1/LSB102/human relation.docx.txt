Conflict and stress are major problems arising in organizations which can have a massive negative effect in the management of the organization. When not solved, can have negative influences on the institution’s development across the members of the organization. Common goals and aims planned and achieved for many years can be ruined in a small period of time if actions relating to the solution is not attended to immediately to address the conflicts and stress in the organization. The following are strategies I would use to address the conflicts and stress within my team as the team leader.
To begin with, I would encourage communication. Communication is very important when addressing conflicts and stress within a team. By facilitating open communication, promoting active listening, providing opportunities for feedback, utilizing non-violent communication, and providing conflict resolution training, team members can work together more effectively towards their collective goals while maintaining a positive and productive work environment.
I would try and identifying the source of the conflict. I would take appropriate steps such us gathering information, looking for patterns, identifying underlying needs and interests, consider the organizational context etc. to address the underlying issues and work towards a resolution. This can help to create a more positive and productive work environment for everyone involved. Also, I would find common a ground, team members can work together to address conflicts and stress in a way that meets everyone's needs. This is done by identifying the issue, finding shared goals, brainstorming solutions, evaluating options and agree on a solution. This approach encourages collaboration and fosters a positive and productive work environment.
Furthermore, I would set clear expectations, this is another way for addressing conflicts and stress within a team is crucial to create harmonious and productive working environment. By following these steps, identify the issues, define the goals, develop strategies, assign responsibilities, implement and evaluate, and provide ongoing support in doing that you would develop a comprehensive plan for addressing conflicts and stress within your team. With a clear plan in place, you can create a more positive and productive work environment, and help your team achieve the goals of the team and that of theirs too. 
Again, I would provide support to the team members. This is an important strategy for managing stress within a team. When your team members feel supported, they are more likely to communicate effectively, resolve conflicts in a constructive way, and manage stress in a healthy manner. This is done by making yourself available, offering resources, fostering teamwork, providing feedback, and leading by example, you would help team members effectively manage their challenges and work together more productively.
Finally, I would be monitoring our progress and celebrate with them when there is improvement. Monitoring our progress and setting milestones helps to ensure that the strategies put in place are effective and that progress is being made towards resolving the issues. Here, setting clear goals, identifying key performance indicators, regular check-ins, analyzing feedback, and celebrating successes would help to create a more positive and productive work environment, where team members can work together effectively towards a common goal.
After all the strategical processes discussed above; encouraging communication, identifying the source of the conflict, setting clear expectations, providing support and monitor progress when carefully observed would help resolve conflicts and stress ideally among team members in any organization which can help increase the productivity and efficient management of organization.
