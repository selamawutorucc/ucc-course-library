Artificial Intelligence 
1. Artificial Intelligence is a method of making a 
computer, a computer-controlled robot, or a 
software think intelligently like the human mind. 
2. Artificial Intelligence is the theory and 
development of computer systems able to 
perform tasks normally requiring human 
intelligence, such as visual perception, speech 
recognition, decision-making, and translation 
between languages.

3.Price (budget)

Chromebook, Linux)
