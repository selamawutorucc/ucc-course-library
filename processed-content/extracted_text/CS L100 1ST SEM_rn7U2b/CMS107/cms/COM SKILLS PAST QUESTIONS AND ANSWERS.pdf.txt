emotions.                                                                                                                                                
(NB. “It’s about time” always takes the verb in the past)

(Spelling error: rr, ss for embarrassment)

(In spite is not a one-word but separate words)

(We don’t say ‘investigate into’ but instead, ‘investigate; a crime)
