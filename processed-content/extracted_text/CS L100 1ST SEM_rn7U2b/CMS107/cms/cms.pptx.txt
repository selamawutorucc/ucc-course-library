CV OR RESUME
A Curriculum Vitae ( Latin phrase, which means “course of life”) is a detailed document highlighting a person’s professional and academic history. CVs typically include information like work experience, achievements and awards, scholarships or grants a person earned, coursework, research projects and publications, among others. You may be asked to submit a CV when applying for an employment.

--- SLIDE 3 ---
HOW TO WRITE A CV
No one formula for a CV or resume will work for everyone. In a CV or resume, you need to convey to your prospective employer what you have accomplished and when (with dates), with details of your education, work experience, interests, etc. 
You need to show that your qualifications and experience that make you suitable for the job you are applying for

--- SLIDE 6 ---
KEY POINTS TO CONSIDER
Use headings to indicate the main sections
Highlight section headings and important information with boldface, italics, bullets, indentations. Use clear simple design.
Keep your CVs or resumes short if possible. Do not include extraneous information to make the CV longer.

--- SLIDE 7 ---
KEY POINTS TO CONSIDER
Include information and experience relevant to the job you are applying for. 
Use reverse chronological order (that is begin with your most recent education and work experience).
Proofread your CV several times and ask someone else to edit it carefully as well to make sure there are no errors.
