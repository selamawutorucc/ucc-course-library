Limiting the topic
The purpose of limiting  your  topic is to help you produce a topic that you can write about properly and well in one paper.
If your subject is too large, the information you provide to the reader will be too general or vague  hence not interesting:
E.g. Communication in the world
 Three  ways of communicating non verbally 
Narrow the idea to a specific number or aspect so it can be managed.

--- SLIDE 11 ---
THE INTRODUCTORY PARAGRAPH
It is the first paragraph of an essay.
It is supposed to introduce the reader to the essay by welcoming him or her to the topic.
It needs to be interesting to hook the reader to read the essay.
There should be introductory sentence(s) that set the tone for the essay.
It should state the thesis or purpose of the essay by outlining how the ideas will be presented in the essay. ( Clearly states the organization or plan of the whole essay). 

Attention must be given to the introduction because a first impression lasts and the reader is assured that the essay is worth reading if the introduction is good

General importance of the topic



--- SLIDE 14 ---
THE THESIS STATEMENT
It contains the central idea or ideas that the essay seeks to develop.
It controls and limits what the essay intends to cover.
It must be written in specific and precise language.
It is a mandatory content/part of the introduction: 
 This essay will discuss three  causes of teenage pregnancy in Ghana OR This essay will discuss peer pressure, broken homes and poverty as the causes of teenage pregnancy in Ghana.

--- SLIDE 15 ---
THE BODY PARAGRAPH
It provides details in support of the thesis.
It contains a number of paragraphs with each paragraph developing one main point.
Each paragraph of the body paragraph must clearly indicate the topic sentence, the supporting sentences and sometimes a concluding sentence.
Transitional markers or devices, such as, “again”, “furthermore”, “secondly”, “on the other hand”, can be used to link the ideas.
The paragraphs must be arranged logically making sure ideas flow into one another. 
There should be both inter and intra paragraph cohesion.



--- SLIDE 16 ---
THE CONCLUDING PARAGRAPH
Every good essay must have a concluding paragraph and it is an equally important part of the essay so attention must be given to it.
It is the final/last paragraph of the entire essay.
It sums up the main points which were introduced in the essay.
METHODS/TECHNIQUES
A writer can suggest solutions or make deductions.
A writer can project into the future.
A writer can reiterate his/her stand.
A writer can re-state thesis.
A writer can use summary. 

Do not introduce a new point in the concluding paragraph

	It is mostly at the beginning of the paragraph.
Supporting Sentences/Details
Major sentences
Minor sentences
Concluding Sentence
Summary sentence(s) which is optional


--- SLIDE 18 ---
In the end, your essay must satisfy the following conditions:
Completeness: The Thesis Statement must be completely developed in the body paragraphs of paragraphs of development.
Coherence: All the sentences must flow into each other to develop the ideas stated in each paragraph.
Unity: The sentences must work together in developing the ideas in each of the paragraphs.
