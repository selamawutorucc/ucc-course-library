PRONOUNS
Pronouns: words that replace a noun in a sentence to avoid repetition. 

They allow language to flow without interruptions and ensure smooth information is well transmitted.

E.g.. He, she, it etc.

--- SLIDE 5 ---
Personal pronouns
They replace subjects (action performers)
Example: Olumide bought some books.
                    He bought some books.


They also replace object.
 Eg. Olumide bought some books.
         Olumide bought them.


--- SLIDE 6 ---
Possessive,  Reflexives, Relatives
Possessives show ownership
Eg. The books are his.

Reflexives point  back to the subject 
Eg. Olumide bought the books himself.

Relatives are linked in a certain way to the subject
Eg. See the boy who wrote the poem.

--- SLIDE 8 ---
VERBS
Verbs are doing words and can express:

 physical action (e.g., to walk, to eat).
  mental action (e.g., to meditate, to think).
  state of being (e.g., to live, to be).

The verbs of being need more attention. We use them but are often not conscious of them as verbs.

--- SLIDE 10 ---
Forms of verbs
Infinitive form: simplest forms (preceded by to) eg. to come, to eat, to sleep, to jump

Past tense: expresses past action. Eg. came, ate, slept, jumped

Present tense: present action. Eg. comes, eats, sleeps, jumps

Future: future action. Eg. Will come, will eat, will sleep, will jump


--- SLIDE 11 ---
Transitive/intransitive
Transitive verbs: can take indirect objects (give, buy, sell)
Eg. Zinzin bought Dondon a book.

Intransitive verbs: cannot take indirect objects (eat, jump, sleep)
Eg. Dondon jumps.



--- SLIDE 12 ---
Active/Passive sentences
Active: subjects of verbs perform the actions. 
Eg. Zinzin eats pawpaws.
        Sophie writes the poems.

Passive: subjects (grammatical) do not necessarily do the actions.
Eg. Pawpaws were eaten by Zinzin.
        The poems were written by Sophie.

Verb conjugation
A verb changes form to agree with its subject. It can either be singular or plural. There are 3 forms of singular/plural verbs: 1st, 2nd, 3rd persons: 

Everyday I come.                1st Singular 
Everyday you come.         2nd Singular 
Everyday she comes.        3rd Singular
Everyday we come .          1st Plural
Everyday you come.         2nd Plural
Everyday they come.        3rd Plural
