TITLE   An experiment to investigate the ’s law of cooling for a liquid.
AIM    To verify Newton’s law of cooling for water.
APPARATUS
Copper calorimeter with outer jacket, copper stirrer, thermometer (0C - 100C), beaker, ice, analytical balance (±0.01g), electric heater
DIAGRAM
THEORY
METHOD
A calorimeter is placed on a wooden board (bad conductor) and placed near an open window. Room temperature is measured using a thermometer and recorded as 1C. Water is heated in a beaker to 90C, using an electric heater. The calorimeter is filled with the heated water to within a centimeter from the top and the lid is put on top. The thermometer and the stirrer are put in place. Keeping the cooling water well stirred, the temperatures at one minute intervals were taken till it drop to 35C. The room temperature was recorded again at the end of the experiment with a thermometer and was recorded as 2C. The results were recorded in a tabular form. The mean room temperature was obtained by finding the average of 1C and 2C.
