DETERMINATION OF THE ACCELERATION DUE TO GRAVITY USING THE                                           
                                     MATHEMATICAL PENDULUM
AIM: To determine the oscillation period as a function of the cord length and also, to determine the acceleration due to gravity.
APPARATUS:  Light barrier with counter, power supply (5V DC/2.4 A), steel ball with eyelet (d=24.4mm and32mm), meter scale, demo,(l=1000), cursors,1 pair, thread (l=100m), right angle clamp, clamping pads on stem, support rod, tripod base, stop watch.
DIAGRAM: 
METHOD:  The apparatus was set-up as shown in the diagram above. The steel ball was tied to one end of the thread and the other end was fixed in the clamping pads on the stem. The light barrier was switched on and it was made sure that the counter read zero. The ball was then displaced at a very small angle α and allowed to oscillate  through the light barrier counter with a thread length of 60cm. The stop-watch was used to time 20 oscillations (when the counter read 40,since the light barrier was used to measure a half cycle). The procedure was repeated for four other values of the length (l), of the thread. The results were tabulated and a graph of Period, T, as ordinate and  , as abscissa was plotted.
