PROCEDURE 
The experiment was set up as displayed in the manual. The resistances (wire resistances) to be 
investigated are shown in the diagram above. 
Resistor Q and the standard resistor R were connected as shown on the diagram using the connection box. 
The power supply was switched on and the correction of the connections was checked by touching the 
bridge wire at the extreme ends X and Y with the slider J. Observing negative and positive readings on 
the digital multimeter G confirmed the correct connections. The balanced point was located on the wire at 
J, the position where there is zero reading on the digital multimeter. 
The length l1 was measured from the end point X to the balanced point and also l2. The experiment was 
repeated with the resistance wires of different diameters. The resistances of Q in each of the wires are 
calculated. Also for each wire, the cross-sectional area was determined. The results were tabulated.

DISCUSSION 
Using the diameters of the wires indicated on the resistance wire board, the cross-sectional areas were 
determined in the formula 
   
  where d is the diameter of the wire. The length of the wire was divided by 
the cross-sectional areas respectively. Also from the slide wire measuring bridge, the resistance of the 
wires RX was also determined by multiplying the standard resistor of 10Ω by the ratio of l1 and l2. When a 
graph of RX against l/A was plotted, it produced a straight line passing through the origin (0,0). The slope 
was found to be 0.506 x 10-6Ωm and since the slope is the resistivity in the formula given in the theory 
above, it means the slope is the resistivity of the material of the wire. In other words, the resistivity of the 
material of the wire is 0.506 x 10-6Ωm. 
 
SOURCES OF ERROR 
1. 1. Most of the time the balanced point fell in a range of about 10mm and so the precise 
point could have been different from the point recorded. 
 
PRECAUTIONS 
1. It was always made sure that the jockey firmly touched wire throughout the reading so as 
not to obtain a wrong value for the resistance. 
2. The connections were always checked after each reading ensure consistent correct value.
