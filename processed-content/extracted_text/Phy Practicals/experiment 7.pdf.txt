THEORY 
Light can travel through vacuum as well as other material such as glasses, air and water. The 
atoms of a material absorb, reemit or scatter the light. Therefore the speed of light in any

--- PAGE 2 ---
material is slower than in vacuum and the speed of light depends on the nature of the material. 
Refraction is the change in the direction of light ray when it travels through different materials. 
The index of refraction of a material describes the extent to which the speed of the light in a 
medium differs from that of vacuum. Lenses are optical instruments made from transparent 
materials that refract light. They refract light such that the image of the source of light is formed. 
An object at infinity which is centred on the principal axis of a converging lens produces parallel 
rays to the principal axis which eventually converge at the focal point of the lens after passing 
through the lens. A converging lens has a curved surface with a thickened centre unlike a 
diverging lens. The distance between the focal point and the lens is the focal length, f. It is 
assumed that the lens is thin as compared to   such that it makes no difference whether   is 
measured between the focal point and either surface of the lens or the centre of the lens. The 
converging lens can form both real and virtual images depending on the position of the object 
from the lens.  When an object is located at a distance from the lens greater than twice the focal 
length, the image is small, real and inverted than the object. An object located a distance from 
the lens that is between the focal length and the twice the focal length, the image is real inverted 
and larger than the object. An object within f produces virtual, upright and larger image as 
compared to the object. The refractive power of a lens is the reciprocal of the focal length in 
metres.

PROCEDURE 
The approximate focal length of the converging lens was obtained by focusing the image of a 
tree outside the lab on the screen. The illuminated object O and the screen were placed on the 
optical bench about more than 4f apart and the separation l was recorded carefully. Placing the 
lens nearer the screen, its position was adjusted until a sharp image was focused. The position Y 
of the lens on the bench was noted and recorded. The lens was moved away from the screen until 
a, at a second position X, a magnified image was formed on the screen and sharply focused. The 
difference between the two readings X and Y was denoted as the displacement d. The experiment 
was repeated for about five different values of l and the d values were noted.

DISCUSSION 
From the equation in the theory, it was indicated that l2-d2 was directly proportional to l hence a 
graph with a straight passing through the origin was produced. The straight line in the graph had 
a slope of 0.80m. From the theory it was realized that the slope of the graph was about four times 
the focal length of the lens. The focal length measured at the initial phase of the experiment was 
20cm. From the graph, the focal length was also calculated to be 0.20m or 20cm. so it can be 
observed that the experimental value of the focal length was equivalent to the calculated focal 
length. Hence, this proves the formula accurate.  
 
SOURCES OF ERROR 
1. Error may be due to the fact that the meter rule was not accurately calibrated and 
therefore gave wrong values for the measurement of the displacement (d),x and y 
distances. 
2. Error may be due to the fact that the lens was not made from a material which is highly 
transparent so that all the light rays can be refracted perfectly to a single focus to form 
sharp images. The inability to distinguish very sharp images from somehow distorted 
ones resulted in measuring wrong positions as where the sharp images were formed.     
 
PRECAUTIONS 
1. It was ensured that the illuminated object and the screen were separated more than 
4f before measuring the distance between them in order to ensure accuracy. 
2. It was ensured that the illuminated object was bright in order to form clear images 
to avoid errors.
